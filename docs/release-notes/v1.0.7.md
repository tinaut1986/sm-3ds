- New OPTIONS -> PLAY -> REDUX: Super Metroid Redux's changes in one switch (one missile opens a red door, faster doors and lifts, quick morph, Space Jump in water, a wider X-ray and more). The "?" beside it lists them all.
- New options AUTO SPACE JUMP (hold jump in a spin to keep space jumping) and AIR SPIN (press jump in the air to start spinning, as in Zero Mission).
- New OPTIONS -> SOUND: louder by default (200 %, up to 400 %), with MUSIC and EFFECTS on their own bars.
- HEAVY PHYSICS: the wall jump rises like a normal jump, Hi-Jump is no longer too high and water and lava feel as in Redux.
- X-ray scope with WIDE: no more doubled background, bright strip at the top or broken cone, also in 3D.
- Your CLASSIC buttons stay as set when you load a save state, and Screw Attack can be reached again in the equipment screen without the Spring Ball.
